Arcana — Privacy

by Lightly

Everything stays on your phone

Everything you write in Arcana stays on your phone. There is no account and no cloud. Nobody can read what you write — not even we who built the app. We run no servers that store your data, no analytics, no trackers, and no advertising.

Two narrow exceptions

Two narrow exceptions use a language model. For daily counsel, everything sent is translated into plain library symbol-words on your phone first: the quality-words being worked (“Warmth”), today's public sky, colour and advice-category names, craft facts as fixed library words, and short excerpts of counsel the model itself generated so it can avoid repeating itself — nothing you wrote. Your placements (“Sun in Cancer”) ride along only while the stars interest is on; never your birth date, time, or place. Advanced Astrology is separate: once per UTC day, the relay calculates the public planetary positions and asks for one reading in all four languages. That call contains no user, device, birth or app data — only date-derived sky facts and Arcana's classical source notes — and the same reading is stored for everyone. Offline, daily counsel speaks from the built-in library and Astrology uses today's cached reading when one exists.

Never your desire in its own words. Never a word you have written. The daily counsel request passes through our relay, which stores nothing about you, to a language-model provider; no account identifier, name, or device identifier travels with it.

Fixing failures

If an Arcana call fails, the phone sends one small technical report so we can fix it: what went wrong, how long it waited, the app build, the platform and the language. For daily counsel, it also carries which fixed wizard model answered, the candidate lane, request and response character counts and structure, response status, and the fallback or seal decision. It never carries your writing, counsel, birth data, or anything that identifies you.

Your record is yours

You can export everything the app holds as one readable file, at any time, from Settings — and delete it all just as easily. If you delete the app, the record goes with it; we could not recover it for you even if you asked, because we never had it.
