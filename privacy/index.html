Arcana — Privacy

by Lightly

Everything stays on your phone

Everything you write in Arcana stays on your phone. There is no account and no cloud. Nobody can read what you write — not even we who built the app. We run no servers that store your data, no analytics, no trackers, and no advertising.

The one exception, off by default

One exception: each morning, when your phone is online, the app asks a language model for the day's counsel, once a day. Everything sent is translated into plain symbol-words on your phone first: today's sky, the quality-words being worked (“Warmth”), the single quality-word beneath your mission, a short stock phrase for your morning note's tone (“ease — no rushing”), the area of life calling loudest, and the app's own correspondence notes. Your astrological placements (“Sun in Cancer”) ride along only while “weave my sign” is on in the app. Offline, the app speaks entirely from its own built-in library.

Never your birth date, time, or place. Never your desire in its own words. Never a word you have written. The request passes through our relay, which stores nothing, to a language-model provider; no account identifier, name, or device identifier travels with it.

Your record is yours

You can export everything the app holds as one readable file, at any time, from Settings — and delete it all just as easily. If you delete the app, the record goes with it; we could not recover it for you even if you asked, because we never had it.
